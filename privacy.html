TINY PLANET

Privacy Policy

Tiny Planet, by Grindhouse Digital. Effective November 6, 2026.

This policy explains what information Tiny Planet (the "Game") collects when you play it on Meta Quest or PC VR. In short: the Game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not have permission to use the internet, so nothing you do in the Game is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small file on your headset or PC. It holds your game progress (for example your farm, crops, chickens, coins, home and your farmer's look) and your settings (for example music and sound volume, graphics quality and comfort options). This file stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Headset and controller data

The Game uses your headset and controller positions while you play so it can draw the world and let you point at menus. This data is used only in the moment on your device. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Meta and other platforms

If you buy or download the Game through the Meta Horizon Store, Meta handles your purchase, your account and any information it collects under its own privacy policy. When the Game starts, it asks the Meta software on your headset to confirm that your account owns the Game; this check is handled by Meta, and we do not receive any of your account information from it. We do not receive your payment details. Please see Meta's Privacy Policy for how Meta handles your information.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the Game collects, we will update this page and the date above before that update is released.
