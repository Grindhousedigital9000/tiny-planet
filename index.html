A cozy little farming world, just for you.

Somewhere out there is a tiny planet, and it's all yours. Farm, explore, chat with your neighbours, or just play with your chickens and hunt for new breeds. There's no winning and no losing. Plant crops, watch them grow, make your farmer your own and your house a home, and grow your planet one new biome at a time, all held right in front of you in VR.

Features

  • A whole round planet you can hold, spin and zoom from a full view down to your farmer's front door.
  • Till, plant, water and harvest; sell at Jade's stall and offer coins at the shrine to grow new biomes.
  • Raise a flock, catch wild chickens and cross breeds to discover new ones.
  • Fish, forage, and light your way at night with a lantern among glowing mushrooms.
  • Customize your farmer at the Looking Glass and furnish your home.
  • Comfort options and a play-anywhere tabletop view, seated or standing.
  • Fully offline. No accounts, no ads, no data collected.
